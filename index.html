One bucket of biryani,Karachi, 1969.

In October 1969 Haji Muhammad Ali set up a stall on Katrak Road in Karachi with a few plates and one bucket of rice. Students queued, then generations queued. The same layered biryani is cooked fresh every day in JVC — chicken, mutton, tikka and the smoky barbecue one.

Practical

Before you order.

Taken from the branch listing and the brand's own site, not from guesswork.

What time are you open?

Daily from 11 AM to midnight. The kitchen takes its last orders around 11:45 PM.

Where is the JVC branch?

Mulberry 1, Emirates Garden 2, Shop #1, District 14 — on the JVC circle. The plus code is 26X4+X5 Dubai and the map below drops you at the door.

Can I get it delivered?

Yes — order direct on studentbiryani.ae, or through Talabat, Zomato or Deliveroo. The branch covers JVC and the streets around the circle.

What should a first-timer order?

Chicken biryani with raita, a haleem to share, and naan. If you want the table to go quiet, add the mutton karahi.

Is there anything vegetarian?

Veggie biryani AED 20, veggie karahi AED 20, daal fry and kadhi pakora AED 18, mix veg AED 18. The daal is cooked fresh through the day.

Do you cater for events?

Biryani buckets, family packs and trays of karahi or haleem for 5 to 50 people. Call 04 554 6215 a day ahead and the branch will price it.

Which other branches are there?

In Dubai: Al Karama, Silicon Oasis, Al Barsha and JVC. Elsewhere: Al Reef, Shabiya and Corniche in Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, and Al Rashidiya in Ajman.
